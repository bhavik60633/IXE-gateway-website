FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What Makhana grades do you export? +
We export four grades of Makhana: 4 Suta (Regular/Industrial), 5 Suta (Commercial/Good), 6 Suta (Premium), and 7 Suta (Super Premium/Gourmet). Each grade differs in size, roundness, colour, and intended application.
What is the minimum order quantity? +
We cater to a range of order sizes, from smaller trial shipments to large bulk orders. Please contact us with your requirements and we will advise the minimum order quantity based on the grade and destination.
Can you provide custom packaging and private labelling? +
Yes, we offer fully customizable packaging solutions including private labelling, branding, and nitrogen-flushed packs as per international standards. Standard export sizes are 5 kg, 10 kg, 20 kg, and 25 kg, but custom sizes can be arranged.
Is third-party inspection available? +
Yes, we fully support independent third-party inspection before shipment. This ensures product quality, quantity verification, and compliance with international standards. We encourage buyers to use third-party inspection for quality assurance.
What is the shelf life of your Makhana? +
Our Makhana has a shelf life of up to 12 months when stored in proper conditions — cool, dry place away from moisture and direct sunlight. Shelf life may vary depending on packaging type and storage conditions.
Which countries do you export to? +
We export to international markets across the globe including Europe, North America, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, Australia, and Africa. Our goal is to serve any buyer looking for premium Makhana from India.
How long does it take to get a quote? +
We typically respond to enquiries within 24 hours on business days. To speed up the process, please include your required grade, quantity, packaging preference, and destination port in your enquiry.
Do you handle all export documentation? +
Yes, we manage the complete export process including all necessary documentation, compliance with export regulations, and coordination for smooth international shipments. We ensure a streamlined experience from order to delivery.
