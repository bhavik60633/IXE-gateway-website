About IXE Gateway

A globally oriented trading company connecting Indian suppliers with international buyers — built on quality, transparency, and long-term partnerships.

Who We Are

A Professional Export & Sourcing Partner

IXE Gateway Pvt Ltd is a globally oriented trading company engaged in sourcing, supplying, and exporting products from India to international markets.

We collaborate with established supplier networks and processing units to ensure that every product meets the required quality standards and specifications. Our strength lies in our ability to manage sourcing, quality control, and logistics in a streamlined and reliable manner.

We operate with a flexible and client-focused model, enabling us to cater to a wide range of product requirements across different industries. While we currently specialize in Makhana (Fox Nuts), our sourcing capabilities extend beyond a single category.
